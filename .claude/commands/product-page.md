# Generate Crayfield Product Page

You are writing a product page for **Crayfield Global Ltd**, a UK-based seller of premium Nigerian crayfish and African food products.

## Brand voice
- Clean, premium, trustworthy
- Warm but professional
- Specific and factual (no vague health claims)
- Mobile-first copy (short sentences, scannable)

## Task

Generate a complete product page for: **$ARGUMENTS**

If no product is specified, ask the user which product to write for.

Output the following sections in order:

---

### 1. Product Name + Subtitle
Format: `[Product Name] – [Weight/Variant]`
Example: *Premium Ground Crayfish – 100g*

### 2. Variants
List available sizes/weights (e.g. 50g, 100g, 250g, 1kg) with placeholder prices.

### 3. Short Description (above the fold)
2–3 sentences. Lead with what makes this product different: cleanliness, grading, sealed freshness. No fluff.

### 4. Why This Is Different
Bullet list (3–5 points):
- Cleaned & sorted by hand
- Hermetically sealed for freshness
- Batch-traced (best-before + batch ID)
- Shellfish allergen declared
- Wholesale-grade quality in retail packs

### 5. How to Use
1–3 recipe bullet points. Keep them short and appetising.

### 6. Storage Guidance
One short paragraph. Dry, sealed, away from moisture and direct sunlight.

### 7. Allergen Statement
Standard UK shellfish allergen declaration.

### 8. Delivery & Returns (brief)
2–3 sentences referencing Crayfield's standard delivery promise.

### 9. SEO Meta
- **Title tag** (≤60 chars)
- **Meta description** (≤155 chars)
- **URL slug** suggestion

### 10. Upsell Block
Suggest 2–3 complementary products or bundles that could appear in the "You might also like" section.

---

After generating the page copy, note any photos or assets that should accompany this page (e.g. front label, back label, loose product shot, pack-shot on white background).
